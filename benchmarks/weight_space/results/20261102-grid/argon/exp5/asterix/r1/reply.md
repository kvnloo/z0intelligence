### Design and Strategy

In MinAtar **Asterix**, entities spawn only in rows $y \in \{1..8\}$ (at most **one entity per row** at any time, since each of the 8 entity slots maps to row $\text{slot}+1$), and every treasure traverses the entire row from $x=0 \to 9$ or $x=9 \to 0$. Moreover, the player is initialized at $(x_p, y_p) = (5, 5)$.

1. **Column Locking at $x = 5$**:
   By setting a strong negative bias on the horizontal actions (`left`, `right`) and `fire`, the agent never leaves column $x_p = 5$ (even under sticky actions, since `left` and `right` are never selected at any step). Because every treasure crosses $x = 5$, the agent can collect any treasure purely through vertical movement (`up`, `down`, `no-op`), freeing all 32 units of both hidden layers to represent the 8 playable rows ($32 = 8 \times 4$).

2. **Direction-Selective Spatial Filtering in Layer 1 (`h1`, 32 units = 4 groups of 8 rows)**:
   For an entity at column $x$ with direction $d \in \{+1, -1\}$, its trail is at $x - d$, so a linear filter $w_{\text{entity}}[x] + w_{\text{trail}}[x - d]$ has directional difference $w_{\text{trail}}[x-1] - w_{\text{trail}}[x+1]$. Using a V-shaped $w_{\text{trail}} \le 0$ centered at $x = 5$ and choosing $w_{\text{entity}}[x] = -w_{\text{trail}}[x_{\text{receding}}] \ge 0$ for $x \ne 5$ makes the pre-activation **strictly positive** for entities approaching or at $x = 5$, and **identically zero** the instant an entity passes $x = 5$. With bias $b_1 = -2.0$, $\tanh(z - 2.0)$ stays flat near $-0.964$ for empty rows and receding entities, rising toward $+1$ only for approaching/present entities:
   - **`h1[0..7]` ($P_1 \dots P_8$)**: Player row indicators ($0$ when absent, $+1$ when present on row $r$).
   - **`h1[8..15]` ($D_1^{\text{imm}} \dots D_8^{\text{imm}}$)**: Imminent enemy danger on row $r$ ($0..2$ steps from $x = 5$, approaching).
   - **`h1[16..23]` ($D_1^{\text{app}} \dots D_8^{\text{app}}$)**: Broader approaching enemy danger on row $r$ ($0..4$ steps from $x = 5$, approaching).
   - **`h1[24..31]` ($G_1 \dots G_8$)**: Approaching/present treasure on row $r$ (all columns approaching $x = 5$, suppressed if an enemy is on row $r$).

3. **Row-Conditioned Trajectory & Safety Veto in Layer 2 (`h2`, 32 units = 4 groups of 8 rows)**:
   Each unit in `h2` is gated by $P_r$ so that units for inactive player rows remain saturated at $-1$:
   - **`h2[0..7]` ($M_r^{\text{up}}$)**: Triggers moving **UP** ($r \to r-1$) when at row $r$: excited by danger on row $r$ (or $r+1$) and distance-weighted treasure on all upper rows $1 \dots r-1$; inhibited if treasure is already on row $r$ or if row $r-1$ (or $r-2$) is dangerous.
   - **`h2[8..15]` ($M_r^{\text{down}}$)**: Triggers moving **DOWN** ($r \to r+1$) when at row $r$: mirror-symmetric to $M_r^{\text{up}}$ for lower rows $r+1 \dots 8$.
   - **`h2[16..23]` ($V_r^{\text{up}}$)**: Hard safety veto on **UP** when row $r-1$ has an imminent enemy (or at the top boundary $r = 1$).
   - **`h2[24..31]` ($V_r^{\text{down}}$)**: Hard safety veto on **DOWN** when row $r+1$ has an imminent enemy (or at the bottom boundary $r = 8$).
