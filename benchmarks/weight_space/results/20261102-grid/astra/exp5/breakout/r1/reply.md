The policy predicts both legal catch positions, including wall reflections. It moves into this safe interval and waits. On the final step, it prefers the catch that preserves horizontal direction. While the ball rises, it anticipates a return from the lowest occupied brick row above it, or from the ceiling.

The first layer computes target offsets, motion direction, and brick-row conditions. The second layer selects the applicable movement. Large output weights make action sampling nearly deterministic.
