The policy is a hand-crafted reactive controller:

- First hidden layer detects exact player row, exact danger row, player column scalar, target/diver column scalars, gauges, and target/diver presence.
- Second hidden layer has three sets of row-conditional danger detectors: danger above, danger below, and danger in the current player row. It also builds action-preference units for up/down/left/right/fire.
- Output layer combines these: danger above discourages up, danger below encourages up, current-row danger encourages up and firing; firing requires both a target and current-row danger. Surfacing requires both low oxygen and a diver onboard. Default movement centers toward targets/divers and avoids dangerous rows.
