## Problem

The older framing of a fixed global hierarchy such as deterministic→fly→JEV→SLM→frontier is too architectural.

The stronger invariant is:

> repeated cognition may move to the **cheapest verified mechanism that preserves the registered outcome contract**, and must deoptimize when its empirical validity assumptions stop holding.

## Contract

Every promoted specialist/routine/cascade should carry:

- capability / decision-family identity;
- evidence and state representation version;
- training/dev/sealed/future cohort identities;
- supported operating region;
- calibration evidence;
- verifier/outcome contract;
- cost vector;
- explicit invalidators;
- drift monitors;
- fallback target;
- demotion/deoptimization reason;
- lineage to the stronger mechanism it replaced.

Candidate mechanisms may include rules, caches, retrieval, classifiers, MB/fly learners, SLMs, JEV-style backends, or future mechanisms.

## Acceptance criteria

- promotion is mechanism-neutral;
- validity assumptions are explicit enough to detect a violation;
- invalidation forces fallback without pretending the compiled result remains calibrated;
- drift/future evidence can demote a previously credited candidate;
- training/maintenance/drift cost is included in the economics;
- simpler mechanisms remain mandatory controls.

## Architectural migration

Demote “army of flies” and the fixed cognition ladder from product architecture to **candidate mechanism families** evaluated by Evolution Lab.

## Falsification

If compiled mechanisms rarely remain valid long enough to repay discovery/training/verification/maintenance cost, downward compilation should be narrowed rather than treated as a universal thesis.

## Non-goals

- deleting fly/MB experiments;
- prescribing a universal mechanism order;
- allowing the learner to change its sealed judge.
