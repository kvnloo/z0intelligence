# z0intelligence brand background

Working product name: **z0intelligence** (package/CLI remain `z0int` until a deliberate rename cutover).

## Play on words

**z0intelligence** is a deliberate pun on the “stochastic parrot” critique of neural language models.

We do **not** claim to know whether LLMs, SLMs, or neural nets are “intelligent” in a strong sense.
We do observe that they are becoming extraordinarily useful for **abundance** — turning scarce human attention into higher throughput on real work.

## The problem Kerdoios + z0intelligence solve

A ~$200/month frontier subscription today buys an absurd amount of labor-like capability and throughput.
It also **runs out too quickly**: context, tokens, latency, and careless routing burn the budget before the useful work is done.

- **z0intelligence (z0int)** — local intelligence control plane: typed decisions, specialists, evidence/receipts, backend selection (Laya fast path, Jev reference, mushroom specialists, OpenJev, …).
- **Kerdoios** — residual compute / token allocator: spend frontier only when local routes are insufficient.

Together they aim to keep the useful capability while stretching the budget: local first, frontier when justified, verified outcomes over ambient completion.
