# Scoped SLM methods: replay findings and gates

These methods are observe-only. Historical, small matched replays did not establish a general winner: pairwise ranking lost accuracy to binary on both evaluated families, three-sample voting used roughly three times the tokens without an accuracy gain, and hierarchy improved one family while losing on another. Missing contexts and predictor failures must remain in the denominator. Historical proxy costs are not causal recovery costs or evidence of task success.

A later powered search improperly filtered its training vocabulary using evaluation labels. Its negative result is historical, not a valid training-only evaluation or a promotion claim. The corrected runner constructs its categories only from calls and receipts before the earliest evaluated call or receipt; unseen labels remain in the denominator, and wrong predictions do not become abstentions. A fresh matched measurement is required.

The search separates development selection from holdout evaluation. Candidate publication requires a complete matched binary incumbent, at least 400 unique matching holdout labels and a holdout expected-cost interval wholly below the incumbent's. A published candidate is not a promotion or execution authorization. Graph legality must be pinned independently for each atomic job; a missing graph leaves that job pending rather than inventing legal actions. An explicitly empty graph is a recordable abstention.

Runner database, session directory, endpoint and candidate home are explicit inputs. Session-level manifests and outputs remain private; do not commit raw traces or personal data. No model loading, hook activation or deployment is implied by this source. Measured inference, independent holdout and promotion gates remain separate prerequisites.
