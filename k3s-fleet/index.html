01 What we're building

Canary releases, but for models.

The shadow network. Small local models learn to impersonate Jev, our fast judgment model, on narrow tasks: tool calling, routing, classification. Each one runs in shadow until its evals reach at least 95%. Only then does it take real traffic.

The training data is five years of AI and harness conversation logs. They're labelled and replayed through the shadow network to find what each small model is good at, then fine-tuned.

The workload is thousands of short experiments (replays, evals, fine-tuning runs, builds, test suites), with about 20 agent "leads" submitting jobs at the same time.

02 The fleet

Two k3s nodes, two phones, and a queue nobody uses.

How a job actually runs

    Kueue is installed with three ClusterQueues, factory, lanes and z0, but they're empty. Jobs actually run through a host-lane wrapper: fleet-build checks heat, memory and disk, then starts a systemd-run scope on the build laptop. They never become Kubernetes Jobs.

    The phones run Linux inside PRoot on Android: no cgroups, no netlink, no systemd. They can't run a kubelet, so they're host-lane workers with battery and thermal gates.
